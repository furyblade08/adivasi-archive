Kanhu Murmu

Kanhu Murmu remains one of the most remembered figures in Santhal political history and collective memory.

Overview

Overview

Kanhu Murmu is historically associated with the Santhal community and with the movement that became central to anti-colonial resistance in eastern India. His name is part of the archive of community memory and political struggle.

Historical significance

Historical significance

He is remembered as a leader whose role in collective action, resistance and political memory continues to shape the historical story of Adivasi communities in the region.

Community & region

Community / region

Santhal community, especially in the eastern Indian region associated with the Santhal rebellion and its legacy.

Movement

Major movement or contribution

Kanhu Murmu is remembered for his role within the Santhal movement and its wider campaign against exploitation and loss of community rights.
