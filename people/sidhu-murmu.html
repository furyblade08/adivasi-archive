Profile

Sidhu Murmu

Sidhu Murmu is remembered in Santhal history as one of the principal leaders associated with resistance and the memory of community struggle in eastern India.

Overview

Overview

Sidhu Murmu stands as a key figure in the historical memory of the Santhal community. He is associated with the wider struggle against exploitation and colonial pressure in the region.

Historical significance

Historical significance

His name remains central to the Santhal uprising and the long record of organised resistance in the Chota Nagpur and surrounding regions. He is remembered for his place in community memory and public history.

Community & region

Community / region

Santhal community, especially in what is now Jharkhand and the wider eastern Indian belt.

Movement

Major movement or contribution

Sidhu Murmu is remembered for participation in the Santhal rebellion and for his role in shaping the political memory of resistance, community solidarity and survival.
