Overview

Oraon community overview

The Oraon, also known as Kurukh, are an Indigenous community spread across Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, West Bengal and Assam. Their culture centers on village ritual, craft, and shared community life.

  • Community: Oraon (Kurukh)
  • Regions: Eastern India
  • Language: Kurukh

Language

Kurukh language

Kurukh is the principal language of the Oraon community and belongs to the Dravidian family. It is written in Tolong Siki and also recorded using other regional scripts.
