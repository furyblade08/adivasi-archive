Overview

Ho community overview

The Ho are an Austroasiatic-speaking community concentrated in Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal and parts of Bihar. Their cultural life includes seasonal ceremonies, communal farming and ritual knowledge.

  • Community: Ho
  • Regions: Eastern India
  • Language: Ho

Ho language

Ho is an Austroasiatic language spoken by the Ho community and is used in daily life, song and ritual. It is written in Latin script and in local writing systems for literature and education.
