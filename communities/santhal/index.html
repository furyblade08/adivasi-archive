Santhal

An Adivasi community of eastern and central India with a rich history, language and cultural heritage.

Overview

Santhal community overview

The Santhal are an Indigenous community with deep roots across eastern and central India, especially in Jharkhand, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha and Assam. Their identity is shaped by village life, agricultural practice, oral tradition and a strong cultural connection to land.

  • Community: Santhal
  • Regions: Eastern and central India
  • Language: Santhali

Language

Santhali language

Santhali is the principal language of the Santhal community and belongs to the Munda subgroup of the Austroasiatic family. It is written in Ol Chiki as well as other regional scripts used by speakers across different states.

Ol Chiki: The Ol Chiki script is an important writing system for Santhali literature and education, used alongside other scripts in the region.
