Bhil

The Bhil people are one of the oldest Indigenous communities in western and central India, known for forest knowledge, archery and traditional arts.

Overview History Culture Language Photographs Documents Articles
Overview

Bhil community overview

The Bhil are an Indigenous community with a long presence in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Telangana. Their identity is rooted in forest-based livelihood, ritual practice and communal knowledge.

  • Community: Bhil
  • Regions: Western and central India
  • Language: Bhil languages
