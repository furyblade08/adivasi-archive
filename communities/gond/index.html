Gond

The Gond community is known for forest-based livelihoods, storytelling and expressive visual culture in central India.

Overview

Gond community overview

The Gond are one of the largest Indigenous communities in central India, with significant populations in Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Maharashtra, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Their identity is shaped by forest livelihood, storytelling and artistic culture.

  • Community: Gond
  • Regions: Central India
  • Language: Gondi

Language

Gondi language

Gondi is the principal language of the Gond community and belongs to the Dravidian family. It is written in a variety of regional scripts and is also shared orally across generations.
